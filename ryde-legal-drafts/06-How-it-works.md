# How it works

*In-app page · DRAFT v2, 4 October 2026*

**Two sides of the same country: booking one, and listing one.**

## Getting a ride

Open the app and it shows taxis online within 5 km of you. Say where you are going, and you see the fare **before** you commit. The first driver to accept is yours, and you watch them come.

A set fare is the fare you pay. A fare marked as an estimate is confirmed by the driver before you set off. In San Pedro fares are set by zone. On the mainland they are metered at BZ$3 per mile, with a BZ$7 minimum.

Pay the driver **in cash** at the end. Card payment is coming once Belize Bank's payment page is live.

## Renting a vehicle

Pick your dates and see what is actually free for that window. Every price is **all in**: delivery, collection, GST and, for carts, the bridge pass. Fuel and damage are not included. Longer is cheaper: a three-day deal beats three single days.

The shop brings the vehicle to you, checks your driving licence, and walks it round with you noting fuel and damage. There is a cash deposit (BZ$200 by default), held by the shop and returned when the vehicle comes back as it left.

## Changing your mind

Cancel more than 24 hours before handover and it costs nothing. Inside 24 hours, or if you are not there, it costs one day's rate. All of this is shown before you book.

## Becoming a driver

Account, then **Become a Ryde driver**. Upload your taxi permit, PSV driver's licence and passenger insurance. A person reads each application. You will see where yours is. Once approved, go online when you like. **You pay Ryde nothing.**

## Listing your shop

Account, then **Become a Ryde partner**. Shop name, handover point, WhatsApp number and one vehicle to start. Once approved, set your own rates, add your fleet and take bookings. Ryde takes 15% of completed bookings and you keep the rest. While payments are cash you collect the full amount and settle the commission separately.
