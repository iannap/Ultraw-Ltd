6.1 **Licence and age.** You must be 18 or over and hold a valid driving licence, and show it to the shop at handover. A foreign or international licence is accepted. A shop may set a higher minimum age and may refuse handover if your licence is missing or expired.

6.2 **The rental period.** A rental day is 24 hours from handover. Delivery, collection and, for golf carts, the bridge pass are included in the rate. Fuel and damage are not.

6.3 **Payment.** Today, payment is **cash** to the shop. The reservation fee is **not a deposit and is not deducted from what you owe the shop**.

6.4 **Deposit.** A shop may take a refundable cash deposit (BZ$200.00 by default) at handover. It is returned when the vehicle comes back as it left. If a shop keeps part, the reason is recorded in the app and shown on your receipt.

6.5 **Cancellation and no-shows.** See the Refunds and Cancellations policy, which forms part of these terms. In short: free more than 24 hours before handover; one day's rate inside 24 hours; one day's rate for a no-show. These are shown on the booking screen before you commit.

6.6 **Your responsibilities.** A golf cart is a motor vehicle under the Motor Vehicles and Road Traffic Act, Cap. 230. Every road rule that applies to a car applies to you. Do not drive after drinking alcohol or taking drugs. Carry no more people than there are seats. Do not take a vehicle off its island or out of its permitted area. You are responsible for damage, loss and traffic fines during the rental, other than fair wear and tear.

6.7 **Insurance.** A shop must carry third-party insurance as the Motor Vehicle Insurance (Third Party Risks) Act, Cap. 231 requires. That cover normally does **not** cover damage to the vehicle itself, theft, or injury to you or your passengers. Ask the shop what cover applies **before** you drive away. Ryde does not insure you.
