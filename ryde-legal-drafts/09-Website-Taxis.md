# Taxis in Belize

*Published at ryde.bz/taxi (replaces ryde.bz/taxi-san-pedro, which should redirect) · Search title: Taxis in Belize, Ryde · DRAFT v2, 4 October 2026*

Ryde books taxis in towns across Belize, starting in San Pedro, Ambergris Caye. Set where you are and where you are going, see the fare, and a driver comes to you.

## How it works

1. Open Ryde and tap **Where to?**
2. Choose your pickup, whether your location, the airstrip, the water taxi dock or your hotel, and your destination.
3. See the fare **before** you book, then confirm.
4. Follow your driver on the map until they arrive.

## Made in Belize to help Belizeans earn more

Ryde takes **no commission** from taxi drivers and adds **no booking fee** for riders. The fare you see is the fare. Every fare and every tip goes to your driver.

## Drivers with their papers in order

Every driver on Ryde drives their own taxi and has shown a current taxi permit, a PSV driver's licence and insurance that covers passengers. A driver whose papers lapse cannot go online until they are renewed.

Golf carts on Ryde are rentals you drive yourself. They never carry paying passengers.

## The fare

In San Pedro fares are set by zone. On the mainland they are metered at BZ$3 per mile with a BZ$7 minimum. A surcharge may apply late at night. The fare is shown before you book. You pay your driver in cash; card payment in the app is on its way.

## Questions

**How much is a taxi?** It depends on the distance or zone. Ryde shows the fare for your trip before you book, so there is no haggling at the end.

**Can I get a taxi from the airport or the water taxi?** Yes in San Pedro: the airstrip and the water taxi dock are pickup points.

**Are the drivers licensed and insured?** A driver cannot go online without a taxi permit, a PSV licence and passenger insurance on record and in date. Ryde checks the documents; it does not itself license or insure anyone.

**How do I pay?** In cash to your driver. Card payment is on its way.

**Can I tip?** Yes, and the whole tip goes to your driver.
