# About Ryde

*In-app page · Ryde Belize, Ryde Tech, Belize · DRAFT v2, 4 October 2026*

**Made in Belize to help Belizeans earn more. Ryde takes nothing from taxi drivers and adds nothing to riders' fares.**

## Why Ryde exists

Belizeans deserve services that are convenient and reliable, and a way to earn that does not take a cut of every hard-working day. So a taxi driver pays Ryde **nothing**: no commission, no sign-up fee, no weekly fee. **Every fare and every tip goes to the driver, in full.**

A rider pays what the ride costs. Ryde adds no booking fee and no service charge on top.

## How Ryde pays its way

Rental shops pay Ryde **15% of each completed booking** and keep the rest. That is the only money Ryde takes today.

If that ever changes, it will be said here first. **Any charge on drivers needs 60 days' notice**, and drivers may leave without penalty.

## What it is

Taxis in towns across Belize, booked from your phone, with the fare shown before you ask for the ride. Golf carts to rent on the islands (San Pedro now, Placencia planned), delivered to where you are staying. Car rentals on the mainland are coming.

## Who you are dealing with

The taxis belong to their drivers and the vehicles to local shops, **not** to Ryde. Each is a Belizean business responsible for its own licence and its own insurance. Ryde checks that a driver has shown a current taxi permit, driver's licence and passenger insurance before going online.

For anything about a vehicle, the driver or the shop is the fastest answer. Their number is on your trip or booking.
