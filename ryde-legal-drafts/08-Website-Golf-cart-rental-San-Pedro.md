# Golf cart rental in San Pedro

*Published at ryde.bz/golf-cart-rental-san-pedro · Search title: Golf cart rental in San Pedro, Ambergris Caye, Ryde · DRAFT v2, 4 October 2026*

On Ambergris Caye the golf cart is the car. Ryde puts San Pedro's rental shops in one place: pick your dates, see every cart's all-in price, and have it brought to your hotel or house.

## How it works

1. Open Ryde and go to **Rent**. Pick when you need the cart and where you are staying.
2. Every cart shows its total for those dates, the rate, delivery and 12.5% GST together, so you compare like with like.
3. Book, and the shop confirms with you on WhatsApp.
4. The cart is delivered to you, and collected when you are done.

## What the price includes

- Delivery to where you are staying, and collection at the end.
- The bridge pass, so you can drive north of the river.
- 12.5% GST, shown in the total.

Fuel and damage are **not** included. The shop takes a cash deposit at handover and returns it when the cart comes back as it left.

## What you need

A valid driver's licence carried while you drive; to meet the shop's minimum age (18 or over); and to understand that a day is 24 hours from handover.

## Golf carts are for renting

Carts on Ryde are rentals you drive yourself. They never carry paying passengers. For a ride, book a taxi.

## 4 or 6 seats

Each listing shows its seats and a photo, and says so when the photo is of the model rather than that exact cart.

## Questions

**How much does a golf cart cost in San Pedro?** Each shop sets its own rates, by the hour, day, three days or week. Ryde shows the full price for your dates before you book.

**Can I drive north of the bridge?** Yes. The bridge pass is included.

**Is fuel included?** No. Neither is damage.

**How do I pay?** The shop, in cash, when the cart arrives. Card payment is on its way.

**What if I cancel?** Free more than 24 hours before handover. One day's rate inside that window or for a no-show.

**Do I need a licence?** Yes. A valid driver's licence, checked at handover.

**Is it insured?** Shops carry third-party insurance as Belize law requires. That does not normally cover damage to the cart, theft, or injury to you. Ask the shop **before** you drive away.
