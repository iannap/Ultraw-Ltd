## The vision, as the documents now state it

> Ryde takes nothing from taxi drivers and adds nothing to riders' fares. Ryde earns from rental shops, and from honest partnerships, not from people who are working hard to make a living.

That promise appears in the Terms, the Driver Agreement, About Ryde, and the Taxis page. It is written as a **commitment with a stated exception process**: if Ryde ever changes this, it must give 60 days' written notice and a driver may leave without penalty. This is deliberate. A promise with no way out is a trap for you; a promise that can change in secret is a trap for them.

## Decisions I need from you (they change the text)

1. **Card fees on taxi fares.** When a rider pays a taxi fare by card, Belize Bank will charge Ryde a processing fee. Who bears it? The drafts say **Ryde bears it**, because that is the only way "the driver gets the full fare" stays literally true. If you cannot afford that, the promise must be reworded to "no commission", and a clause added. Tell me which.
2. **Cancellation and no-show fee**: one day's rate each. Keep, or lower for locals?
3. **Reservation fee** equals the 15% commission and is non-refundable. A Belize attorney may say that is a penalty. Decide whether to keep it.
4. **Minimum age**: 18 to rent, 18 to ride alone. Confirm.
5. **Dispute resolution**: courts of Belize (drafted) or arbitration.
