# Driver Agreement (Taxis)

*Ryde Belize, operated by Ryde Tech, Belize · BCCAR No. 000059715 · DRAFT v2, 4 October 2026, for attorney review*

**Plain-language summary.** You drive your own taxi, you set your own working hours, and **Ryde takes nothing from your fares or tips**. In return you keep your papers current and you drive safely and honestly.

## 1. Parties and status

1.1 This agreement is between **Ryde Tech** (**Ryde**) and you, an individual driver (**Driver**).

1.2 You are an **independent business**. This is not employment, a partnership, agency or franchise. You decide whether, when and where to work; you may decline any ride; you may work for others, including competing platforms; you use your own vehicle, phone and tools; and you are responsible for your own tax and insurance. Ryde does not give you instructions on how to drive or which routes to take, beyond the safety and conduct rules below.

1.3 **[ATTORNEY TO CONFIRM: Ryde sets taxi fares and offers rides by distance. Does that put independent status at risk, and what practice or wording protects it? See covering brief item 3.]**

## 2. Our commitment on money

2.1 **Ryde charges you no commission, no sign-up fee, no weekly fee and no other charge for using the Service as a taxi driver.**

2.2 **Every fare and every tip is yours in full.** Today you collect the fare in cash from the rider. When card payment goes live, a card-paid fare is collected through Ryde and **paid to you in full**. **Ryde bears Belize Bank's card processing cost on taxi fares. [OWNER DECISION: confirm.]** Payout timing: **[within 2 working days]**, to the bank account you give.

2.3 **Changing this.** Ryde may introduce a charge on drivers only by giving you **at least 60 days' written notice** in the app and by email. You may end this agreement at any time, without penalty, before the change takes effect.

2.4 **Fares.** Ryde sets the fares shown to riders: set zone fares in San Pedro, and on the mainland **BZ$3.00 per mile with a BZ$7.00 minimum**, plus any posted late-night surcharge. The fare is shown to the rider before they book. You may agree a lower fare with a rider. You may not charge more than the fare shown, except for agreed extras such as tolls or waiting where the app allows.

## 3. What you must hold, and keep holding

3.1 To go online you must hold, and have recorded in the app:
 (a) a **taxi permit** for your vehicle;
 (b) a **public service vehicle driver's licence** of the right class;
 (c) **insurance that covers passengers**, as the Motor Vehicle Insurance (Third Party Risks) Act, Cap. 231 requires, naming your vehicle's plate; and
 (d) the **prescribed identification mark** on your vehicle under the Motor Vehicles and Road Traffic Act, Cap. 230, s.6(1). **[ATTORNEY TO CONFIRM the prescribed format; the plate format is deliberately not stated here.]**

3.2 All must be **in date**. If any lapses, **you cannot go online until it is renewed and uploaded**, and you must stop taking rides when it lapses.

3.3 You confirm each is genuine and yours. A false document is grounds for immediate removal and reporting to the authorities.

3.4 Ryde keeps copies of your documents in private storage, visible to administrators. A photo may be read by a Cloudflare AI service to fill the form; you may check and correct the result. See the Privacy Policy.

3.5 Ryde checks documents on upload and on expiry. **Ryde does not licence, insure or certify you.** Riders rely on you to keep your papers current.

## 4. Taking rides

4.1 Ryde offers each ride to taxis within **5 km** of the pickup. The **first driver to accept** gets it.

4.2 Accept only rides you will complete. Repeated cancelling or no-shows may lead to suspension.

4.3 Take the rider to the destination by a reasonable route. Keep your vehicle clean, roadworthy and safe. Obey every road rule. **Never drive after drinking alcohol or taking drugs.** Carry no more passengers than your vehicle's seats.

4.4 **Golf carts are never used for paid rides.** The Service gives you no way to dispatch one. Do not use a rented cart to carry paying passengers.

4.5 Treat riders with respect. No harassment, discrimination or violence. No unlawful items.

## 5. Records and location

5.1 The app records your route while you are on a trip, and your location while you are online, for safety, fare accuracy and disputes. See the Privacy Policy.

5.2 You may not share a rider's details with anyone except as the law requires.

## 6. Insurance and liability

6.1 You are responsible for your vehicle, your driving, your insurance and your compliance with Belize law.

6.2 Ryde is a platform. It does **not** insure you, your vehicle or your riders. A claim arising from a ride lies between you, the rider and your insurer. Ryde will hand over the trip records it holds to either side, or to an insurer, who properly asks.

6.3 You agree to indemnify Ryde against claims, losses and costs arising from your driving, your vehicle, or your failure to hold the permits, licences and insurance in section 3, **except to the extent caused by Ryde's own fault**. **[ATTORNEY TO CONFIRM enforceability.]**

6.4 Nothing here limits liability that cannot lawfully be limited.

## 7. Ending this agreement

7.1 You may stop at any time. Please finish rides you have accepted.

7.2 Ryde may suspend or remove you at once if you break section 3 or 4, put riders at risk, or give false information. Otherwise Ryde will give **14 days' notice** and a reason, and you may reply and ask for a review.
