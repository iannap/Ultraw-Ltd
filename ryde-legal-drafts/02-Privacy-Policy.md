## 3. Reading your documents with AI

When a partner uploads a photo of a licence or permit, the photo is sent to a **Cloudflare AI service** which reads the number and dates so the form can be filled in. A person can see and correct the result. **[ATTORNEY TO CONFIRM: Cloudflare's retention terms for this service have not been reviewed. Until they are, consider turning this feature off.]** You may type the details in yourself instead.

## 4. Who can see your information

- A **shop** sees bookings for its own vehicles, and the booking rider's name and phone.
- A **driver** sees pickup, drop-off, name and phone for a ride they have accepted.
- **Riders** see shops, vehicles and prices, not other riders.
- **Ryde administrators** see marketplace activity and partners' uploaded documents, to run, support and keep the Service safe.
- Access limits are enforced by the database itself, not only by the app.

## 5. What we do not do

We do **not** sell your data, use it for advertising, or send marketing messages you did not ask for.

## 8. Deleting your account

You can delete your account in the app. We will remove your personal details. **The financial ledger is append-only: rows cannot be changed or deleted.** So some records of completed rides, bookings, commission and payments **must stay**. We keep them in a form that identifies you as little as we can **[ATTORNEY TO CONFIRM that this meets the Act]**.

## 9. Your rights and choices

You have the right to know what we hold, to have it corrected, and to have it deleted where we have no lawful reason to keep it. You may withdraw consent at any time. You can edit your name, phone and photo in Account; refuse location permission (you will then pick your pickup point by hand); and switch off notification categories for **requests, payments and partner applications**. Messages about your own trips and bookings **cannot** be switched off, because you need them to ride or rent.

To ask for a copy of your data or its deletion, email info@ryde.bz. We will reply within **30 days**.

## 10. Children

The Service is for people **18 or over**. We do not knowingly collect children's data. **[ATTORNEY TO CONFIRM 18 as the minimum.]**

## 11. Security and breaches

We use access controls, encryption in transit, and private storage for documents. If a breach puts your data at risk we will tell the Belize data protection authority without undue delay, and tell you where the law requires.

## 12. Changes and complaints

We will show notice in the app before a material change. If you are unhappy with how we handle your data, tell us first at info@ryde.bz; you may also complain to the Belize data protection authority.
