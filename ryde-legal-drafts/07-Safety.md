# Safety

*In-app page · DRAFT v2, 4 October 2026*

**What is actually checked, what is not, and who to call.**

## In an emergency

Call **911** first and tell us afterwards. An app is not an ambulance.

## What Ryde checks

- **Drivers.** A driver cannot go online without a taxi permit, a PSV driver's licence and passenger insurance on record and in date. Papers are checked on upload and when they expire. **Ryde relies on the documents shown and does not licence or insure drivers.**
- **Rentals.** Before handover the shop records your licence number and expiry and may refuse if it is missing or expired. The vehicle is walked round with you at handover and return, noting fuel and damage.
- **Trips.** The route is recorded while a driver is on a trip, to help settle any dispute.

## What is not settled yet

**Insurance on rented vehicles.** Whether a vehicle rented to a visitor is covered, and under whose policy, is still being settled with a Belize broker. Ryde carries no insurance of its own. Ask the shop what cover applies **before** you drive away. Taxis are different: a driver cannot go online without passenger insurance in date.

## Riding and driving in Belize

- Never drive after drinking.
- Wear a seat belt where one is fitted. Most golf carts have none and nothing between you and the road.
- Children sit inside the vehicle, not on a lap or the back step.
- Night roads, especially north of the bridge on Ambergris Caye, are dark. Slow down. Sand and rain together make a road slick.

## Something wrong?

For anything about a vehicle or driver, call the driver or shop first, as their number is on your trip or booking. For anything about Ryde, email info@ryde.bz or message WhatsApp +501 613 5737. Report a safety concern in the app and we will act on it.
