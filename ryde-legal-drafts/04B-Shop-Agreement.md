# Shop Agreement (Rentals)

*Ryde Belize, operated by Ryde Tech, Belize · BCCAR No. 000059715 · DRAFT v2, 4 October 2026, for attorney review*

**Plain-language summary.** You list your golf carts or cars on Ryde, set your own rates, and take bookings. Ryde takes a commission on each **completed** booking. Rentals only: you may not use Ryde to carry paying passengers in a cart.

## 1. Parties and status

1.1 This agreement is between **Ryde Tech** (**Ryde**) and the business that applies to list (**Shop**).

1.2 The Shop is an **independent business**. This is not employment, a partnership or a franchise. It is not exclusive: the Shop may take bookings anywhere else. The Shop owns, insures and runs its vehicles and is responsible for its staff.

## 2. What the Shop must hold, and keep holding

2.1 The Shop confirms when it lists, and for as long as it stays listed, that it holds:
 (a) **a business licence** to rent vehicles from the authority that applies to its location, for example, **a current San Pedro Town Council trade licence** for golf-cart rental on Ambergris Caye. Trade carried on at more than one premises may need a separate licence for each (Trade Licensing Act, Cap. 66, s.6(5));
 (b) **a valid permit for every golf cart listed**, where one is required, from the authority that issues them (on Ambergris Caye, the Ambergris Caye Traffic Control Committee). A cart without its permit can be fined **up to BZ$2,500** and impounded;
 (c) **third-party insurance in force** on every vehicle, as the Motor Vehicle Insurance (Third Party Risks) Act, Cap. 231 requires; and
 (d) for mainland car rental, whatever **registration, licence and insurance** the law requires for that business. **[ATTORNEY TO CONFIRM.]**

2.2 **Moratorium.** Golf-cart rental trade licences and new imports for rental are said to have been suspended from **23 October 2024**. **[ATTORNEY TO CONFIRM whether this is still in force.]** It is the Shop's own duty to check what applies to it. **Ryde does not advise on it and gives no assurance.**

2.3 The Shop tells Ryde at once if anything in this section lapses. Listing without it puts the renter, the Shop and Ryde at risk, and is grounds for **same-day removal**. Ryde may ask for proof at any time, and an administrator can see the documents uploaded.

## 3. Rentals only

3.1 Golf carts are listed **for self-drive rental only**. They must never be used to carry passengers for hire or reward. Taxi drivers and shops are separate accounts; a cart cannot be offered for rides on Ryde.

## 4. Commission, money and tax

4.1 Ryde charges the Shop **15% of the total of each completed booking**. The Shop keeps the rest. The percentage is a setting Ryde may change for **future** bookings on **30 days' notice**. **[ATTORNEY TO CONFIRM: GST on Ryde's commission.]**

4.2 **Cash payments (today).** The Shop collects the whole amount from the renter and **settles the commission with Ryde** within **[7 days]** of the booking completing. Ryde keeps an append-only record of commission owed and paid. **Rows cannot be changed or deleted.**

4.3 **Card payments (once live).** The renter pays Ryde only a **reservation fee equal to Ryde's commission**. The Shop collects the balance, GST and any deposit **in cash**. If the reservation fee is refunded to the renter (Refunds policy, section 3.3), Ryde bears that.

4.4 The Shop sets its **own rates**. Ryde never changes a price without telling the Shop. Rates shown to renters **include GST**; the Shop is responsible for accounting for GST and its other taxes. **[ATTORNEY TO CONFIRM.]**

4.5 **Sponsored listings.** Ryde may, **on terms agreed in advance in writing**, show a Shop first in the rental list for a fee. A listing is marked as **sponsored**. No sponsorship terms are in force today.

4.6 **Partner hotels.** Ryde may tag a guest's rentals from a hotel's front-desk QR code for 30 days. **No payment terms exist today**; none will bind the Shop without its written agreement.

4.7 **Non-circumvention.** The Shop must not tell a renter to cancel and rebook outside Ryde to avoid commission on a booking made through Ryde.

## 5. Bookings

5.1 Confirm or decline promptly. **Honour what you confirm.** Repeated cancelling may cost the Shop its listing.

5.2 At handover check the renter's driving licence and record its number and expiry in the app. Record fuel and existing damage before the vehicle leaves and again on return, using the app's checklists.

5.3 The same cancellation rules apply as to renters: free more than 24 hours out, one day's rate inside that window and for a no-show. The app records the amounts. Collecting them is between the Shop and the renter.

5.4 The Shop may take a refundable cash deposit (BZ$200.00 by default), returns it when the vehicle comes back as it left, and records the reason for any amount withheld.

5.5 Damage and deposit disputes are between the Shop and the renter. Ryde may provide the records it holds but **does not adjudicate**.

## 6. Photos and details

6.1 Photos and descriptions the Shop uploads must be its own, of its own vehicles. The Shop gives Ryde a **non-exclusive licence** to show them in the app and website and to promote Ryde. The Shop may remove them, or its shop, at any time; promotional uses already made need not be recalled.

## 7. Liability

7.1 Ryde provides the platform only. When a renter crashes a vehicle, the claim lies between the renter, the Shop and the Shop's insurer. **Ryde is not a party and carries no cover for it.** Ryde will hand over the records it holds to either side or to an insurer who asks.

7.2 The Shop **indemnifies Ryde** against claims, losses and costs arising from the Shop's vehicles, staff or failure to hold what section 2 requires, **except to the extent caused by Ryde's own fault**. **[ATTORNEY TO CONFIRM enforceability.]**

7.3 Nothing here limits liability that cannot lawfully be limited.

## 8. Ending this agreement

8.1 Either side may end it at any time on written notice. Confirmed bookings should still be honoured, and commission accrued remains due.

8.2 Ryde may remove a listing that is unsafe, unlawful, or repeatedly lets renters down, at once if the risk is serious, otherwise on **14 days' notice** with a reason.

## 9. General

9.1 Governed by the laws of Belize; the courts of Belize have jurisdiction. **[ATTORNEY TO CONFIRM; alternative: arbitration.]**
