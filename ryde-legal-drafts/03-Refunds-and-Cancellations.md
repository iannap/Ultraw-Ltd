# Refunds and Cancellations

*Ryde Belize (ryde.bz), operated by Ryde Tech, Belize · DRAFT v2, 4 October 2026, for attorney review. Forms part of the Terms of Service.*

**Plain-language summary.** Cancel in good time and it costs nothing. Money you paid a driver or shop in cash is refunded by them. Money you paid Ryde is refunded by Ryde to the card you used.

## 1. Rides

1.1 **Before a driver accepts:** cancel for nothing.

1.2 **After a driver accepts:** you may cancel. Please tell the driver, who has made the trip. **Ryde charges you no cancellation fee for rides.** **[Owner to confirm. A driver-set waiting or cancellation charge is not provided for.]**

1.3 If you paid a taxi fare by card (once live) and the ride did not happen or was wrongly charged, Ryde refunds the amount to the same card within **[10 working days]** of confirming the facts.

## 2. Rentals

2.1 **A booking the shop has not yet confirmed** can always be cancelled for nothing.

2.2 **More than 24 hours before handover:** free.

2.3 **Within 24 hours of handover:** one day's rate for that vehicle.

2.4 **No-show:** one day's rate.

2.5 These amounts are the app's current settings and are **shown on the booking screen before you commit**. The fee is worked out on that vehicle's own daily rate. **[ATTORNEY TO CONFIRM: whether one day's rate is a reasonable pre-estimate of loss or a penalty.]**

## 3. The reservation fee (card payment, once live)

3.1 When you pay online to hold a vehicle, you are charged **Ryde's reservation fee**, not the rental. The shop collects the rental, GST and any deposit **in cash** at handover.

3.2 The fee is what makes a shop hold a vehicle for you. It is **not refunded if you cancel**, and the booking screen says so **before** you pay.

3.3 The fee **is refunded in full** if the shop cancels, or cannot give you the vehicle you booked, or if Ryde cancels the booking.

3.4 The fee is not a deposit and does not reduce what you owe the shop.

3.5 A refund of the fee goes to the card you paid with.

## 4. Deposits

4.1 A shop may hold a refundable cash deposit (BZ$200.00 by default). It is returned when the vehicle comes back as it left. The handover and return checklists in the app are the record of condition.

4.2 If the shop keeps any part, the reason is recorded in the app and shown on your receipt.

## 5. If something goes wrong with a vehicle

5.1 A vehicle that will not start, or breaks down through no fault of yours, is the shop's to fix. Call them first.

5.2 If they cannot get you moving and you lose the use of the vehicle, you are entitled to a refund of the value of the time lost. If the shop will not settle it, tell us.

## 6. No licence, no vehicle

6.1 A shop must refuse handover to anyone without a valid licence. That refusal is **not refunded**, because it is the one condition you were told about on the booking screen, in the confirmation, and here.

## 7. Fines and damage

7.1 Traffic fines during your rental are yours, as is damage beyond fair wear and tear. They are charges, not refunds, and are settled between you and the shop, with the app's records available to both.

## 8. How you get paid back

8.1 Money you paid **a driver or shop in cash** is refunded by them, in cash. Ryde does not hold it and cannot return it.

8.2 If they will not refund what they owe you, email info@ryde.bz or message WhatsApp +501 613 5737 with your **receipt or booking number**. We hold the record and will take it up with them. We cannot promise the outcome, but we will reply within **3 working days**.

8.3 Money you paid **Ryde** (card fares, reservation fees) is refunded by Ryde to the card you used.

8.4 These rules do not limit any right you have under Belize consumer law that cannot be removed by agreement.
